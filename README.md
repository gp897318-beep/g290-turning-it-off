One funny single line (98 characters) from a hopeless tech support agent to a desperate customer, written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-motasimfuad-have-you-tried-turning-it-off-154a938b) "Have You Tried Turning It Off?".

## The entry

> I have escalated your case to Tier 2, which is me again, but with a slightly different headset on.

Ten candidates were drafted and compared against the judge's five criteria (on-subject first, then the twist landing on the agent's cheerful uselessness with the customer never the butt of it, then sounding like a real agent line, then tightness, then freshness with a twist on any classic): a haunted ticket restarted three times, hold music while the agent googles the error, Wi-Fi that is fine in an unexplainable way, unplug-count-to-ten with a pretend-first-time, a printer filing a complaint, escalation to Tier 2, a beeping computer more stressed than the caller, the classic reboot with a pact to forget the call, diagnostics blaming the caller's luck, a password expired since 2019. Escalation to Tier 2 won: it is the support trope every caller knows, twisted so the "escalation" changes nothing at all — the same agent, a different headset — which makes the cheerful uselessness the whole joke, keeps the customer out of the punchline, and reads exactly like something a real agent would say while buying time. Runner-up was the haunted ticket ("officially haunted and out of my hands") — its twist is the freshest of the ten, but it explains the joke a beat more than the winner, which simply performs the uselessness and stops.

## Run

node main.ts
